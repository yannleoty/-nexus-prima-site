# Four horizons — artwork brief

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are conceptual illustrations, not photographs, scientific maps or depictions of existing networks of civilisations. The five red connection paths in the final stage are an HTML/SVG overlay representing the proposed network.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one standalone high-quality cinematic astronomical illustration for Nexus Prima, a serious civilisation initiative. Landscape horizontal 16:9, 1536x864 or higher at the same ratio. Realistic physically inspired rendering, restrained editorial aesthetic, deep black background fading seamlessly to pure black at the outer image edges. No text, logos, borders, watermark, UI, humans, spacecraft, mystical symbols or exaggerated neon. Center the main subject at 50% width and 50% height so the image can be smoothly scaled for a website zoom-out. Keep the complete subject inside the frame with generous space around it. This is artwork, not a scientific measurement or map.

## Earth — np-horizon-earth.webp

View of the complete Earth from space, globe centered, diameter about 68% of image height. Recognisable Europe, Africa and Atlantic geography, deep blue oceans, realistic complex thin white clouds, softly illuminated daylight side on upper left, dark night limb lower right, a delicate atmospheric blue edge. Beautiful detailed natural Earth texture. Sparse nearly invisible stars. Realistic and sober, photographic finish. No Moon.

## Solar System — np-horizon-solar.webp

The Solar System seen from an oblique elevated viewpoint. Small bright warm white-gold Sun exactly in the center, diameter 9% of image height with restrained glow; the eight planets at different positions on faint elliptical orbit paths, fully within frame, natural colours, Earth a tiny blue dot, Jupiter larger than other planets but much smaller than the Sun, Saturn with delicate rings. Scientifically inspired composition using compressed distances for visibility, not a to-scale diagram. Not planets lined up in a row. System spread horizontally across 85% width, 65% height. Black space, minimal stars; no massive lens flare.

## Galactic — np-horizon-galaxy.webp

A complete spiral galaxy inspired by the Milky Way seen from outside at a gently inclined angle, centered luminous small ivory core, believable complex spiral arms of countless fine white and blue stars and dark dust lanes. Restrained warm core, subtle natural blue outer arms. Galaxy occupies 78% of width and 65% of height, full outer edges visible fading into black. High-end astrophysical simulation aesthetic, photographic texture. No artificial paths or diagrams. Vast quiet space.

## Universal — np-horizon-universe.webp

An expansive intergalactic view of a group of many distant galaxies scattered in three dimensional deep black space. One small central spiral galaxy at exact image center, only 10% of image width; several farther spiral and elliptical galaxies of varied size and orientation spread throughout the middle 80% of the composition. The remaining points are tiny distant galaxies. Subtle wisps of large scale cosmic structure in a few places, extremely restrained. Ivory and pale gold galactic cores, faint blue-grey starlight, large areas of black. Suggest immense depth and an open universal horizon. Photorealistic scientific-visualisation aesthetic, no giant colourful nebula clouds, no straight diagram connections, no grid, no lasers. Centre visually matches the prior spiral galaxy when zoomed out.
